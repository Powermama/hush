# The hush community

hush welcomes people with different backgrounds, abilities and levels of
experience. Help make the project a place where people can ask questions, learn
and disagree constructively.

Critique ideas and code, not people. Give useful context, respect others' time
and credit their work. Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual
harassment and publishing another person's private information are not welcome.

These expectations apply to project issues, pull requests, discussions and other
spaces managed by hush. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close
disruptive threads, or restrict participation according to the severity and
pattern of behaviour.

Report concerns privately to [hello@behu.sh](mailto:hello@behu.sh). Include links
and enough context to investigate; avoid sharing unrelated personal information.
Reports are handled with discretion and shared only as needed to respond. There
is no guaranteed response time. A report involving a maintainer should identify
that conflict so it can be considered when handling the report.
